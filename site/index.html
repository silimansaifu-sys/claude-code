The quest

Save the world. Collect stickers.
Cook falafel.

The heinous Varakh has his claws on the Staff of Ultimate Power, and the end of the world is penciled into everyone's calendar. Someone has to fix this — and that someone is three kids with a sword, a spellbook, and a very good recipe.

Journey across a land that never takes itself seriously, befriend the bizarre, and stick it to evil. Literally. With stickers.

🏆 Legend says whoever wields the Staff of Ultimate Power gains ultimate power. The name is not subtle. Neither is Varakh.

Battle minigames

Block, dodge, and parry enemy attacks in real time. Every hit you avoid is one you earned.

⭐

Sticker power

Collect stickers on your travels and slap them on for buffs, builds, and bragging rights.

🧆

Falafel cooking

The party marches on its stomach. Cook falafel to keep your heroes fed and fighting.

💡 Pro tip: a perfect parry feels better than any level-up. Practice on the enemies that look huggable. They are not huggable.
